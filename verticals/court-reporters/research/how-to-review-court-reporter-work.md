### 3. Industry Standards and Best Practices
**Certification and Credentials**
- Verify **RPR, CRR, CRC, CSR** licenses matching venue; confirm active continuing education[2][7].
- NCRA Code: Reporters as official verbatim record keepers; explain realtime role clearly; uncertified drafts guided by common sense/professional courtesy[3].

**Quality Control Checklist**
- **Proofreading, formatting review, final certification** post-proceeding[2].
- Check **accuracy** (verbatim capture, error handling/documentation), **consistency** (speaker ID, page layout), **grammar/vocabulary**[2][4].
- Request **sample transcripts** (title page, condensed/word index, exhibit index)[7].
- Punctuality: Arrive early, prepared[4][9].
- Demeanor: Silent unless addressed, unobtrusive[4].
- Ethics: Impartiality, confidentiality, integrity[1].

**Evaluation Process**
- Confirm experience with case types (criminal, civil, family, litigation)[1].
- Test real-time feed via apps for live accuracy check[4].
- Score providers on 100-point scale[7].
- Technical rehearsal for remote: Audio, bandwidth, screen-sharing[7].

**Transcript Review Items**
| Category | What to Check |
|----------|---------------|
| Accuracy | Verbatim testimony, error corrections documented, 95-97%+ benchmark[2][4][5] |
| Formatting | Speaker ID, page layout consistency, indices (word, exhibit)[2][7] |
| Completeness | Oaths, sidebars, sealed exhibits per local rules[7] |
| Security | Data handling, errata sheets[7] |

### 4. Common Pain Points and Solutions
- **Pain: Errors affecting admissibility/appeals** – Solution: Multi-layer QC, certified reporters, sample reviews[2].
- **Pain: Inconsistent formatting across cases** – Solution: Standardized processes, provider transparency[2].
- **Pain: Delays from unpunctual reporters** – Solution: Early arrival verification, experience checks[4][9].
- **Pain: Remote tech failures** – Solution: Pre-rehearsals for audio/bandwidth[7].
- **Pain: Inexperienced reporters in complex cases** – Solution: Match to similar prior cases (e.g., federal procedures)[4][7].
- **Pain: Flawed records in high-volume firms** – Solution: Multiple reviews, real-time monitoring apps[2][4].
