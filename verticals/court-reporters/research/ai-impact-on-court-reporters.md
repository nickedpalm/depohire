### 1. Key Statistics and Data Points
- Court reporting jobs increased by only **2%** between 2014 and 2024, per U.S. Department of Labor, amid rising demand and declining supply of trained stenographers.[7]
- **42%** of legal firms used AI technologies in 2025 (up from **26%** in 2024); **42%** expect increased AI use in 2026, primarily for document review, predictive analysis, litigation strategy, and transcript summarization.[6]
- **741** AI-related bills introduced in U.S. state legislatures across **30 states** as of January 27, 2026, per National Conference of State Legislatures.[3]
- Significant decline in court reporting students and graduation rates; experienced professionals retiring, exacerbating nationwide shortages.[1][2][7]
- No large-scale AI job displacement projected in legal industry for 2026; BLS explicitly states AI cannot replace court reporters.[2][3]

### 2. Current Pricing/Rates
- AI-powered digital court reporting offers **up to 50% cost savings** compared to traditional stenographic services, due to lower transcription costs for routine recordings and faster processing.[1][7]
- No specific 2026 hourly or per-page rates listed; digital reporting reduces barriers via shorter certification times versus stenographic training.[7]

### 3. Industry Standards and Best Practices
- Certified digital court reporters obtain certification through **American Association of Electronic Reporters and Transcribers (AAERT)**, ensuring standards for accuracy in monitoring speakers, exhibits, objections, and non-verbal cues.[7]
- Hybrid model: Combine **digital recording systems** (e.g., JAVS in Kentucky courts as official record), **AI for real-time captioning/transcription**, and human transcriptionists for certified transcripts.[1]
- **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)** with AI transcription: Digital reporter oversees, edits errors, notes events; used in depositions and hybrid proceedings.[7]
- NCRA recognizes stenographer shortages but emphasizes advanced skills; digital solutions as sustainable for non-certified needs.[1][7]

### 4. Common Pain Points and Solutions
| Pain Point | Details | Solutions |
|------------|---------|-----------|
