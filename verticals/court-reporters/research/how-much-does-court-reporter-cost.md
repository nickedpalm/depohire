### 3. Industry Standards and Best Practices
- **Federal standards (2026)**: Pay levels tied to NCRA certifications—merit (accuracy) and realtime (live transcription). Hired post-Oct 2009 follow tiered scale with locality adjustments (e.g., $81k–$108k Level 1).[1]
- **General**: Postsecondary nondegree award + short-term training standard; no formal experience required per BLS.[3]
- **Related roles**: Court transcriptionists average $96,542/year ($46.41/hour); stenographers $84,999 ($40.86/hour).[4][6]

### 4. Common Pain Points and Solutions
- **Pain point: Low entry pay in digital roles**: SC digital reporters at $41,597/year vs. national $80k; solution—pursue stenographic/realtime certs for federal Level 3/4 ($110k+).[1][7]
- **Pain point: Salary variability**: 25th percentile $44k–$62.5k; solution—target high-cost areas or specialized roles like district court admin ($114k).[4][6]
- **Pain point: Stagnant growth**: BLS projects 0% jobs change (17,700 employed); solution—certifications for merit/realtime to access +5–15% pay bumps.[1][3]
