### 3. Industry Standards and Best Practices
#### Attorney Checklist for Preparation
- **Scheduling**: Propose dates/locations early via letter to opposing counsel after initial discovery; suggest neutral sites; select court reporting service (stenography, real-time, video, remote/mobile)[2].
- **Document Handling**: Review/gather contracts, emails, reports, witness statements, photos/videos, diagrams, interrogatories; organize by deposition outline chapters; pre-mark as exhibits with copies for all parties[1][2][8].
- **Outline Development**: Build deposition outline divided into issue-based chapters (key case facts, strengths/weaknesses, opponent's story); focus on core issues[2][4][10].
- **Witness Prep**: Review facts chronologically; practice brief, fact-based answers; explain procedures (oath, attendees, court reporter/videographer roles); coach on objections, pauses[1][3][4][5][8].
- **Day-Before Checklist**: Confirm time/place/documents; final review; rest[1].
- **Day-Of Checklist**: Arrive early; professional attire (conservative, neutral, no flashy jewelry/gum); organized documents; speak slowly/clearly with pauses[1][3][4][7].
- **Court Reporter Coordination**: Ensure quiet environment; confirm specialized terminology; calibrate equipment with backups; arrive early for setup[5].

#### Room Setup Requirements
- Quiet, distraction-free space; early arrival for equipment (stenography machines, recording devices, video tech); access to exhibits/digital tools; special arrangements for remote (tech checks)[2][5].

### 4. Common Pain Points and Solutions
- **Pain: Unprepared witnesses (nervous, long-winded answers)**. Solution: Practice anticipated questions; focus on brief responses; review facts/timeline deeply[1][3][8].
- **Pain: Tech/Equipment Failures**. Solution: Pre-check stenography/recording devices, backups; test remote/video setups[2][5].
- **Pain: Disorganized Documents/Exhibits**. Solution: Copy/organize by outline order; pre-approve witness docs (ID only unless arranged)[2][8][9].
- **Pain: Objections/Conduct Violations**. Solution: Distribute Civil Rules copies; remind on permissible objections[2].
- **Pain: Poor Demeanor (gestures, expressions)**. Solution: Coach conservative dress/grooming; avoid mannerisms for video[3][4].
- **Pain: Over-Disclosure**. Solution: Stick to facts; attorney prep is privileged—do not repeat[6].
