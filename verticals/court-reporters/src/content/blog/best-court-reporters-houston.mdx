## Practical Bottom Line: How to Actually Choose

1. **Make three calls this week.** Pick U.S. Legal Support, NAEGELI, and either Steno or R.T. Reporting. Describe your typical deposition: "Two attorneys, one witness, 4 hours, we need real-time feed and rough draft by tomorrow." Notice who answers confidently and who hedges.

2. **Ask about certifications.** Texas has certified professional court reporters (CPRTs). They matter. Ask if their reporters are certified and how long they've been doing this. Experience in complex civil or criminal cases matters.

3. **Request references.** "Can you give me the name of a partner at [Big Law Firm] who uses you regularly?" If they balk, that's a sign. Real firms have real clients who'll vouch for them.

4. **Check their tech stack.** Do they offer real-time streaming? Zoom integration? Same-day rough drafts? These aren't nice-to-haves—they're standard. If a firm is still using dial-in lines or charging extra for same-day delivery, you're paying a premium for outdated service.

5. **Book them once with low stakes.** Use your next deposition as a test run. See how responsive they are, whether the transcript is accurate, whether the real-time feed actually worked. One gig will tell you everything.
